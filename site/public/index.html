What it does

Your daily count stays in view, so a few spare minutes turn into reviews.

Cards left, per deck

Pick the decks you care about. New, learning and review are counted the way Anki counts them.

Review in place

Images and audio included. Space shows the answer, 1–4 grade it, and the grade goes to Anki's own scheduler.

Syncs on its own

Every 15 minutes, through Anki and your AnkiWeb login. No new account.

⌃⌥A from any app

One shortcut brings the widget forward and back. Change it in Settings.

On top or on the desktop

Drag it anywhere. Keep it above every window, as a normal window, or down with your desktop icons.

4 themes, 3 sizes

Paper, Midnight, Matcha and Terminal. Full, compact, or a slim pill. The count also sits in the menu bar.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Anki Desk reads from the Anki app on your Mac. Sync uses the AnkiWeb login you already have in Anki.

Will it change my scheduling?

Only when you review. Each grade goes to Anki's own scheduler, exactly as if you'd answered in Anki.

Is it safe to install?

Each release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and all of the source is on GitHub under the MIT license.

What if Anki is closed?

The widget shows the last count it saw and a button to open Anki. It picks up again as soon as Anki is running.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Is it affiliated with Anki?

No. It's an independent, MIT-licensed app that works with Anki through the AnkiConnect add-on.
